Free online math practice

One Minute Math is free online math practice with nothing extra in the way: no ads, no account, no streaks or prizes. Pick a skill, then either race the one-minute clock or work at your own pace.

Timed or untimed

  • Timed: the classic one-minute drill. See how many you get right, then try to beat your own best.
  • Practice: no clock. Good for learning a new level, a single times table, or cooling down after a timed round.
  • Missed questions: a short round built from the facts you missed lately. A fact drops off once you get it right twice in a row.

A simple way to use it

Start with basic math for kids at Level 1, or jump to a suggested level in the practice by grade guide. Spend a few minutes, look at the misses, and stop while it still feels like a win.

Questions

Can I practice math with no timer?

Yes. Choose practice mode for untimed math practice, or timed mode for a one-minute round. You can also print a worksheet.

What kinds of math can I practice?

Whole-number facts, fractions, mixed numbers and decimals, in addition, subtraction, multiplication, division or a mix of all four.

Does the site remember what I miss?

After a round you can redo the facts you missed. The Missed questions option builds a short round from recent misses until you get each one right twice in a row.
