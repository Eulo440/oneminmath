Questions

Is this aligned to a specific curriculum?

No. It is a practical starting point based on when these skills are commonly taught. Use the level that matches what your child is actually learning.

What if my child is above or below grade level?

Ignore the grade label and pick the level that feels slightly challenging but not frustrating. Moving up a year or down a year is normal.

How much practice is enough?

Two or three one-minute rounds a day is plenty. Short and often beats a long session once a week.
