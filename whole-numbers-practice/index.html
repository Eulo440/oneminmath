Whole number practice

Whole numbers are 0, 1, 2, 3 and so on — the numbers with no fraction or decimal part. Whole number practice is the default on One Minute Math, and it is the right place to build basic math facts before moving on to fractions or decimals.

When to leave whole numbers

Once the facts feel automatic, try fractions or decimals. Those modes use the same one-minute or no-clock setup, just with different kinds of numbers. If you are choosing a starting point by age, use the grade-by-grade guide.

Questions

What are whole numbers?

The numbers 0, 1, 2, 3 and so on, with no fraction or decimal part. Whole number practice is the default on One Minute Math.

How do I switch to fractions or decimals?

On the home page, pick Fractions or Decimals under Numbers, or use the fraction practice and decimal practice guides.

Should kids master whole numbers first?

Yes. Quick addition, subtraction, multiplication and division facts make fractions and decimals much easier later on.
