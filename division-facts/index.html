Division facts practice

Every division fact is a multiplication fact in reverse: if you know 6 × 7 = 42, you know 42 ÷ 7 = 6. This division practice locks both in. All answers are whole numbers, so you can focus on the facts.

Tip: think “what times?”

For 56 ÷ 8, ask “8 times what is 56?” If that is slow, the times table for that number needs more practice first.

What about long division?

This page is for division facts, not a walk-through of the long division algorithm. Facts like 56 ÷ 8 are the steps inside long division. When those are automatic, long division is much less work. You can also print division worksheets with answer keys.

Questions

Is this long division practice?

No. These are division facts, like 56 ÷ 8 = 7. They are the steps used in long division. Knowing them by heart makes long division much faster, but this site does not walk through the long-division algorithm.

Should kids learn division after multiplication?

Yes. Every division fact is a multiplication fact in reverse, so a solid times table makes division much easier.

Do any problems have remainders?

No. Every problem divides evenly, so you can focus on recalling the facts.
